Note cards

Four kinds of card, all made the same way, with the question on one side and the answer on the other: a summary of each module, a card for every learning outcome, an equation card that says when to use each formula and what to pull from the case, and a case study card with the details to look for. Read them as sheets, print them as cards to cut and fold, or flip through them on screen. Print them, save them as a PDF, or print a self-test copy with the answers left blank.

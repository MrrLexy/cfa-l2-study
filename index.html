How profiles work

Your progress (the study path, ticked outcomes, what you've read, case-study answers) is saved in this browser under your username. Each username has its own progress and nobody else's.

Because there is no account or server, a profile lives on the device where you made it. To carry yours to another device, choose Export my progress from the profile menu, then Import progress on the other device.

Anyone who types the same name on the same device opens the same profile.
